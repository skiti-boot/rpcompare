RPCompare Stage 8 - Credential Check

Extract into:
C:\Users\King\website

Then run:
python manage.py check_merchant_credentials

This only checks environment-variable presence.
It does not create accounts or contact merchants.

Required Amazon:
AMAZON_CREATOR_CLIENT_ID
AMAZON_CREATOR_CLIENT_SECRET
AMAZON_PARTNER_TAG

Required eBay:
EBAY_CLIENT_ID
EBAY_CLIENT_SECRET

Optional eBay:
EBAY_CAMPAIGN_ID
EBAY_REFERENCE_ID
